Kung Fu Queen

Privacy policy

26 November 2026

Your dojo and save data

Kung Fu Queen saves your dojo name, progress, settings and reward counters on your device. The iOS version has no game account or publisher-operated cloud save. Losing the local save can also lose consumable items and play progress.

Purchases and restoration

Apple processes purchases. The game uses StoreKit to verify signed transactions and keeps transaction identifiers, purchased rights and delivery records locally to restore permanent items and avoid duplicate rewards. The game does not receive your payment-card details. Apple manages its own transaction records.

Optional rewarded ads

Choosing a video reward starts Google Mobile Ads. This TestFlight version uses Google test ads and requests non-personalized ads. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash and performance information for ad delivery, measurement and diagnostics. The included Google consent SDK can process approximate location, performance information and interactions when its services are used. The App Store version uses a production rewarded ad unit. Where Google requires a privacy choice, the game checks consent before requesting a production ad. You can revisit the choice under Settings → Ad privacy when available.

Your choices

You can skip video rewards. Permanent ad removal lets you claim eligible rewards without an ad, subject to the same game limits. Deleting the app removes its local save from that device. Apple and Google manage records they hold under their own policies.

Contact and privacy requests

For game support or a privacy request, email the Kung Fu Queen team. We use your email address and the details you send to investigate and respond. You can ask for deletion of correspondence. Do not send passwords, card details or complete receipts.

Privacy notice for players in Malaysia

Baca notis dalam Bahasa Melayu

Kung Fu Queen saves your progress and purchase entitlements on your device. If you buy something, Apple handles payment and sends the game a transaction record to deliver or restore eligible items. If you choose a rewarded ad, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, performance and diagnostic data to deliver ads, limit repeat impressions, measure results and prevent abuse. Google’s consent service handles applicable privacy choices. Service providers may process data outside Malaysia.

Purchases, rewarded ads and support requests are optional. Skipping an ad leaves the related ad reward unclaimed unless you hold Permanent Ad-Free. If you request help, VENORA Studios receives the email address and details you choose to send. To ask about access, correction or deletion of that information, or to limit its use, contact player support. To change an ad consent choice, use the in-game privacy options when available. Apple handles payment-account requests.

Notis privasi untuk pemain di Malaysia

Kung Fu Queen menyimpan kemajuan permainan dan rekod kelayakan pembelian pada peranti anda. Jika anda membuat pembelian, Apple memproses bayaran dan menghantar rekod transaksi kepada permainan untuk menyerahkan atau memulihkan item yang layak. Jika anda memilih iklan berhadiah, Google mungkin memproses alamat IP dan anggaran lokasi, pengecam peranti, paparan iklan dan interaksi, serta data prestasi dan diagnostik. Data ini digunakan untuk memaparkan iklan, mengehadkan kekerapan paparan, mengukur hasil dan mencegah penyalahgunaan. Perkhidmatan persetujuan Google mengurus pilihan privasi yang berkenaan. Penyedia perkhidmatan mungkin memproses data di luar Malaysia.

Pembelian, iklan berhadiah dan permintaan bantuan adalah pilihan. Jika anda melangkau iklan, ganjaran berkaitan tidak akan dituntut kecuali anda memiliki kelayakan bebas iklan kekal. Apabila anda meminta bantuan, VENORA Studios menerima alamat e-mel dan maklumat yang anda pilih untuk hantar. Untuk memohon akses, pembetulan atau pemadaman maklumat tersebut, atau untuk mengehadkan penggunaannya, hubungi sokongan pemain. Untuk mengubah pilihan persetujuan iklan, gunakan pilihan privasi dalam permainan apabila tersedia. Apple mengendalikan pertanyaan berkaitan akaun pembayaran.
