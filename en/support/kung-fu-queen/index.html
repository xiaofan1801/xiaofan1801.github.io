Kung Fu Queen

Game support

Restore a permanent purchase

Sign in with the Apple Account used to buy the item. Open the game shop and select Restore Purchases. Permanent ad removal, purchased outfits and other permanent rights can be restored. Coins and other consumables do not transfer through this action.

A reward or purchase is missing

For a video reward, wait for the ad to complete and return to the game. Check the reward once. For a pending payment, reopen the game with a connection and check purchase history before trying again. Refund requests for App Store purchases go to Apple.

Report a problem

During the beta, open TestFlight, select Kung Fu Queen and send beta feedback. Include your build number, iPhone or iPad model, language, approximate time, and a screenshot of the problem. Remove Apple Account details and payment information.

  • Apple purchase history
  • Request a refund from Apple

Master Draw ×10 odds

Dojo level 30. Limit one pack per game day. Ten draws per purchase. For each normal draw, Frost, Scarlet and Jade each have a 1/120 chance (2.5% S in total); the remaining 97.5% awards 10 pages. After 49 consecutive draws without an S, the next draw awards each mentor with a 1/3 chance and pages with a 0% chance. The count carries between packs and resets on any S. A duplicate S converts to 20 badges.
